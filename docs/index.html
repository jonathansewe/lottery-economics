    How to read this

    • Expected value (EV) per $1 is the average amount you get back for every dollar you spend, averaged over every possible draw. Anything under $1.00 loses money on average.
    • The advertised jackpot is paid out over 30 years as an annuity. The lump sum ("cash value") is less than half of it. Taxes then take more than half of that.
    • A bigger jackpot sells more tickets, which raises the chance you have to split it. The calculator models this with a Poisson distribution.
    • This site never suggests numbers. Every combination has exactly the same chance.
